2. Winner vs. trade-offs

✔ Where Ultraviolette clearly wins

  • Power & performance — F77's ~30 kW is ~5–6× a typical commuter scooter (Ather 6.4 kW, iQube 3–4.4 kW).
  • Top speed — ~155 km/h (F77) vs 80–90 km/h for most rivals.
  • Range — up to 323 km claimed, highest in the group.
  • Acceleration — 0–100 km/h in 7.8 s; competes with 150–200 cc petrol bikes.
  • Fast charging — 20–80% in ~45 min.
  • Technology — radar safety (X-47), new 100 V (VX-100) architecture, advanced BMS.

✘ Where it does NOT win

  • Price — F77 ₹3.09 L is 2–3× an iQube / 450X / Chetak.
  • Service network — thin outside metros; mainstream brands dominate tier-2 cities like Kurnool.
  • Daily practicality — a performance bike, less convenient than a step-through scooter.
  • Weight — F77 ~207 kg vs ~90–120 kg scooters.
  • Affordability — Ampere, Bgauss, base Ola/Ather win on upfront cost.
  • Scooter today — Tesseract not on sale yet (expected ~Jan 2027).

Verdict: Ultraviolette is "best" if your priority is performance, range, speed and cutting-edge engineering, and you can absorb the higher price and smaller service footprint. For pure budget commuting and wide service access in a city like Kurnool, mainstream brands (Ather, TVS, Bajaj, Hero) remain more practical.
